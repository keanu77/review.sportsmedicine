# Data sources and reuse boundaries

[Project overview](../README.md) · [繁體中文介紹](../README.zh-TW.md) · [Contributor guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

The index combines a rolling upstream catalog with separate bibliography, summary, and classification overlays. It is a selective discovery tool, not a comprehensive systematic review or a clinical appraisal database. Source matching and field completeness do not establish the validity of a study's conclusions.

## What is stored where

| File or content | Source and transformation | Important boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `public/data/reviews-index.json` | Imported from the [upstream public index](https://app.sportsmedicine.tw/data/reviews-index.json); includes titles, identifiers, categories, access hints, and upstream summaries | The complete upstream generation pipeline is outside this repository. A monthly sync can replace this file. Its catalog has a rolling window. |
| `public/data/new-items.json` | Differences between the previous and current index, deduplicated by paper identity | “New” means newly observed in that batch, not necessarily newly published. |
| `public/data/bibliography.json` | Source records matched through Europe PMC; existing compatible bibliography records are retained | Records carry identifiers, source URLs, verification dates, and match methods. Missing or conflicting records stay unresolved. |
| Source abstracts in bibliography | Source-provided text, with original section headings when available and permitted by the public-export rules below | These are distinct from generated Chinese summaries. An excerpt is not a complete abstract. |
| `public/data/summaries.json` | Chinese summaries generated locally from matched source abstracts; newer generated entries include provenance and failed/blocked attempt records | AI output can be wrong and is not a clinician-reviewed recommendation. Older entries may have less provenance. |
| `public/data/tags.json` | Additional clinical topic/population classifications generated by a local model | Tags are search aids. They may contain false matches and do not establish study eligibility or clinical equivalence. |
| `public/data/readiness.json` | Derived source, summary, and metadata completeness counts plus missing-field reasons | “Verified” refers to the indicated source/field check, not study quality or clinical approval. Counts describe the generated snapshot. |
| Journal metrics in the index | Approximate upstream journal impact-factor values with the available JCR-year context | These can be stale and are not a quality score for an individual study. No journal-metrics license is granted by this project. |
| Browser library JSON / RIS / BibTeX | The reader's saved metadata, summaries, identifiers, and reading state | A backup is not a full-text archive and grants no new rights to its source content. Data remains local unless the reader exports or shares it. |

`dist/data/bibliography-index.json` is a build-derived search projection without source abstracts; standalone paper pages carry their own source record. The development server falls back to the full bibliography when this projection is absent.

The bibliography builder is [scripts/build-bibliography.ts](../scripts/build-bibliography.ts). Matching checks DOI/PMID/PMCID compatibility; a title match alone must not overwrite a conflicting paper. Source completeness is computed by [scripts/build-readiness.ts](../scripts/build-readiness.ts). The source abstract cache used for local generation lives outside the publishable repository.

## Reading source and access labels

- **Bibliography source and verification date:** when a source record was checked. This does not mean that the paper's methods or findings were independently assessed.
- **First publication date:** the date supplied by the source. Some source dates may be inferred from a year or month; an index update date is not a publication date.
- **Source-listed free full text:** a compatible source record supplies an access link. The link may later change, and an accessible HTML article does not establish that a PDF is available.
- **Inferred access:** the upstream catalog suggests free access without the same source-link verification. A PMCID or an open-access flag alone does not establish an available full-text link in this implementation.
- **Unknown access or missing summary:** evidence was unavailable or unresolved; the application does not fill the gap with an invented author, result, or recommendation.
- **Study type:** labels inferred from a title are explicitly identified as such. They are not a formal evidence-grade assessment.

## Code, metadata, and article rights

The [MIT license](../LICENSE) covers project code. It does not apply a new license to third-party article content or override the terms of the underlying sources.

**Bibliographic facts and abstracts have different rights.** Crossref describes most bibliographic metadata as factual and reusable, while abstracts can retain publisher or author copyright. Its metadata service is one of the upstream sources; an API response does not automatically place every text field under CC0. [Crossref metadata licensing](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/).

**PubMed and Europe PMC access does not grant blanket republication rights.** NCBI states that journal publishers or authors may hold copyright in PubMed abstracts. Europe PMC likewise requires compliance with the rights and terms applicable to source material. [NCBI policies and disclaimers](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/), [Europe PMC copyright and access guidance](https://europepmc.org/help).

**Full-text reuse depends on the individual article.** Check the license and any exclusions on the source article before redistributing text, figures, or attachments. PMC's open-access subset contains articles with varying license terms. [PMC open-access subset](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/openftlist/).

The public bibliography exporter currently retains complete source abstracts when the source-reported license matches CC0, CC BY, or CC BY-SA. Otherwise it emits an attributed excerpt of at most 25 whitespace-delimited words, marks it `excerptOnly`, and omits section text. This is the project's publication limit, not a blanket legal permission or a guarantee that every downstream use is allowed. License metadata should still be checked against the article. Existing third-party content and AI-generated summaries are not offered as a separately cleared open dataset.

When reusing records, preserve identifiers, source links, available attribution, license information, and the distinction between complete abstracts, excerpts, and generated summaries. Do not infer permission from a “free full text” badge or this repository's MIT badge.

## Corrections and updates

Report a wrong-paper match, missing field, inaccurate tag, or broken link using the [bug/data-correction template](https://github.com/keanu77/review.sportsmedicine/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include the query, DOI/PMID, incorrect value, proposed correction, and an original-source link. Avoid posting a complete copyrighted article or personal clinical information.

The [sync workflow](../.github/workflows/sync-data.yml) describes the monthly update schedule and its retry gates. Current coverage is shown in the site's data-completeness panel and dated [maintenance reports](maintenance/). Those snapshots are more useful than a fixed paper count in the README. Generation and validation commands are in [maintainer operations](maintainer-operations.md).
